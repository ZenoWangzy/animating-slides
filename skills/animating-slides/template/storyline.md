# Storyline: Slides that move

Throughline: a talk deck works when story, scene and sound are timed to the same beat.

Each beat is one step of the deck (`<scene>-<step>`). Check it with
`python3 <skill>/scripts/check-storyline.py storyline.md --deck .`, where `<skill>` is the animating-slides skill folder.

## cover-1
- Say: Most slides sit still while you talk. Today's slides move with you.
- Point: "slides move" — the title and the living characters say it before I speak.
- Source: the speaker

## count-1
- Say: These are thirty days of visitors, one bar a day.
- Point: one bar per day, growing over the month.
- Source: sample data in scenes/count.js (replace with your data file)

## count-2
- Say: That adds up to eleven and a half thousand visitors, someone new every few minutes, day and night.
- Point: "someone new every few minutes" — the total turned into something you can picture.
- Source: computed from the same sample data

## trio-1
- Say: A talk deck is made of three things: the story, the scenes and the sound.
- Point: three cards: Story, Scene, Sound.
- Source: this skill's SKILL.md

## trio-2
- Say: They only work together. Everything flows into your talk.
- Point: three lines flow into one disc, "your talk".
- Source: this skill's SKILL.md

## end-1
- Say: Your turn.
- Point: "Your turn."
- Source: the speaker
